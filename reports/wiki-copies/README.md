# Copies of two wiki pages

These are copies of two pages of the research wiki, as they stood in `work/deep-study/wiki/` on 9 November 2026
(pages last changed on 2-3 November). Each page says of itself that it is `inferred`: a relay of an assistant's
explanations and of lane results, written without opening the lane outputs. Status: unverified.

- `arc-pseudorandom-code-conjecture.md`: Kelvin's conjecture on why ARC's plan may be hard for random deep ReLU nets.
  It is a conjecture, not a theorem. The lanes that bear on it are `cumulant-ambiguity` (its oracle form is false),
  `moment-matching` and the pattern chains in `adaptive-regions`.
- `arc-matching-sampling-bar.md`: what ARC's bar is and what has met it.

Links of the form `[[page-name]]` point to other wiki pages that are not in this repository.
